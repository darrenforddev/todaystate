# TodayState Design Language

Version 1.0

---

# Mission

TodayState exists to help investors understand the markets through intelligence, evidence and clarity.

We do not overwhelm users with data.

We explain what matters.

---

# Core Principles

## Explain before you analyse

Every page should answer:

• What happened?
• Why did it happen?
• Why does it matter?
• Who benefits?
• What should I watch next?

---

## Evidence First

Every opinion should be backed by evidence.

Every conclusion should be explainable.

---

## Connected Intelligence

Everything should connect naturally.

Economy
↓

Themes
↓

Companies
↓

ETFs

Nothing should exist in isolation.

---

## Simplicity Wins

Complex ideas should feel simple.

Simple does not mean shallow.

# Information Hierarchy

Every report follows the same order.

1. Header

2. Scorecard

3. AI Opinion

4. Evidence

5. Risks

6. Investment Options

7. Related Intelligence

# Typography

Bold

Confident

Body text

Easy to scan

Maximum clarity

Avoid long paragraphs.

# Component Philosophy

Every component should have one responsibility.

Example

Only handles evidence.

WhySection

Only displays evidence cards.

ThemeReport

Only assembles sections.
