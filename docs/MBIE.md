# TodayState

## Philosophy

> **Evidence first. Intelligence second. Decisions third.**

TodayState exists to transform market data into explainable investment intelligence.

We believe every investment insight should begin with objective evidence, be processed through transparent reasoning, and ultimately help people make better informed decisions.

We do not chase headlines.

We do not generate unexplained scores.

We do not ask users to trust a black box.

Instead we believe every conclusion should be explainable, every score traceable, and every recommendation supported by evidence.

---

# The Three Principles

## 1. Evidence First

Everything begins with evidence.

Economic releases.

Corporate earnings.

Market data.

Policy decisions.

Interest rates.

Liquidity.

Nothing enters the system without evidence.

---

## 2. Intelligence Second

Evidence becomes intelligence through reasoning.

MBIE evaluates:

• What changed?

• Why it changed?

• Who benefits?

• Who loses?

• How significant is the change?

• How confident are we?

The goal is not to report data.

The goal is to explain it.

---

## 3. Decisions Third

Only after evidence has been analysed should investment decisions be considered.

TodayState does not tell users what to buy.

TodayState provides explainable intelligence so users can make informed decisions for themselves.

---

# Our Promise

Every score should answer:

Why?

Every relationship should be explainable.

Every conclusion should be traceable back to evidence.

If it cannot be explained, it should not exist within TodayState.

---

# The Mission

Transform evidence into intelligence.

Transform intelligence into understanding.

Help people make better investment decisions.

# Market Brain Intelligence Engine (MBIE)

## Vision

The Market Brain Intelligence Engine (MBIE) is the reasoning system that powers TodayState.

Its purpose is not simply to display financial data, but to transform economic evidence into explainable investment intelligence.

Every score displayed within TodayState must be traceable back to supporting evidence.

---

# Core Principles

## 1. Evidence First

Everything begins with evidence.

No score should ever exist without supporting evidence.

Examples:

- ISM Manufacturing
- ISM Services
- CPI
- PPI
- Payrolls
- Retail Sales
- GDP
- Housing Starts
- Yield Curve
- Credit Spreads
- Corporate Earnings

---

## 2. Relationships

Evidence influences investment themes.

Themes influence companies.

Companies influence ETFs.

Example:

ISM Manufacturing

↓

Industrial Recovery

↓

Caterpillar

↓

XLI ETF

---

## 3. Explainability

Every score must answer:

• What happened?

• Why does it matter?

• What changed?

• Who benefits?

• How confident are we?

---

## 4. Dynamic Scoring

Scores are never manually maintained.

Scores are calculated from weighted evidence.

---

## 5. Confidence

Confidence is separate from score.

Score answers:

How positive or negative?

Confidence answers:

How certain are we?
