# Market Brain Intelligence Engine

## Purpose

The Market Brain Intelligence Engine, or MBIE, transforms economic evidence into clear, explainable investment intelligence.

MBIE does not make investment decisions.

It helps users understand:

- What happened
- Why it matters
- What changed
- What may be affected
- How confident the assessment is
- What to watch next

---

# Core Philosophy

> Evidence first. Intelligence second. Decisions third.

Every conclusion must be supported by evidence.

Every score must be explainable.

Every assessment must acknowledge uncertainty.

MBIE changes its view when the evidence changes.
