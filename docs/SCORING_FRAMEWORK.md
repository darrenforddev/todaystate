# TodayState Scoring Framework

Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

The TodayState Score provides a consistent measure of investment attractiveness across all themes, companies, ETFs and sectors.

A score should never be based on opinion alone.

Every score must be supported by evidence.

---

# Score Scale

## 90–100

★★★★★ Exceptional Opportunity

Characteristics

- Strong evidence
- High conviction
- Multiple positive indicators
- Long-term structural growth
- Risks well understood

---

## 80–89

★★★★ Strong

Characteristics

- Positive outlook
- Good evidence
- Attractive opportunity
- Some risks remain

---

## 70–79

★★★ Positive

Characteristics

- Improving conditions
- Mixed evidence
- Worth monitoring

---

## 60–69

★★ Neutral

Characteristics

- Balanced outlook
- Limited conviction
- Await further evidence

---

## 50–59

★ Weak

Characteristics

- Weak evidence
- Increasing uncertainty
- Elevated risks

---

## Below 50

Avoid

Characteristics

- Poor outlook
- Negative evidence
- High uncertainty

---

# How Scores Are Built

The score combines multiple factors.

Current Version

Evidence Strength ............ 30%

Economic Tailwinds ........... 20%

Momentum ..................... 20%

Risk Assessment .............. 15%

Valuation .................... 10%

Market Sentiment ............. 5%

Total = TodayState Score

---

# Principles

Scores should

✓ Be transparent

✓ Be evidence based

✓ Be comparable

✓ Be consistent

Scores should never be changed without supporting evidence.
