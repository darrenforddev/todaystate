# Why TodayState Exists

Markets produce enormous amounts of information every day.

Economic data.

Company earnings.

Central bank decisions.

Commodity prices.

Political events.

Most platforms display this information.

Few explain it.

TodayState exists to bridge that gap.

---

# Our Mission

Transform global evidence into explainable investment intelligence.

---

# Our Vision

To become the world's most trusted explainable financial intelligence platform.

Not by predicting the future.

But by helping people understand the present.

---

# Our Philosophy

Evidence comes first.

Relationships come second.

Intelligence comes third.

Opinion comes last.

---

# What MBIE Does

MBIE is not a prediction engine.

MBIE is an explanation engine.

Its purpose is to answer six questions.

What changed?

Why did it change?

Who benefits?

Who loses?

How confident are we?

What should we watch next?

---

# Confidence

Confidence is earned.

Confidence is never assumed.

Confidence increases when evidence agrees.

Confidence decreases when evidence conflicts.

Confidence is always explainable.

---

# Intelligence

Every conclusion must be traceable.

Every score must be justified.

Every opinion must reference evidence.

Every recommendation must explain itself.

---

# Simplicity

Users should understand the world in minutes.

Not hours.

Complexity belongs inside MBIE.

Clarity belongs inside TodayState.
