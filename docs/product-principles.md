# TodayState Product Principles

## 1. Evidence first.

Every conclusion must be backed by evidence.

---

## 2. Explain every score.

No unexplained numbers.

---

## 3. One page. One purpose.

Every screen answers one question brilliantly.

---

## 4. Build systems, not pages.

Reusable components.
Reusable engines.
Reusable intelligence.

---

## 5. Morning Brief comes first.

Everything else supports it.

---

## 6. Simplicity beats complexity.

If a user cannot understand it in 10 seconds,
it needs redesigning.

---

## 7. MBIE never guesses.

It analyses evidence and explains conclusions.
